Linking production records and controlling changes

江苏中科智芯集成科技有限公司

ERP + iMES connects shopfloor information, parameter changes and production records in this packaging and testing case.

Three problems in the case

  • Disorganized records management.
  • Nonstandard business processes.
  • Low outsourcing and production efficiency.

Digiwin ERP + iMES

The business implemented an integrated Digiwin ERP + iMES solution.

Shopfloor information · SPC

SPC (statistical process control) collects and analyzes shopfloor information in real time.

Parameter changes · ECN

ECN (engineering change notice) distributes process parameter changes in real time.

Linked records

MES links batches, workstations, operators and products/materials for tracing from different perspectives.

Exception recording & escalation

The system automatically opens exception records and escalates them through successive levels.

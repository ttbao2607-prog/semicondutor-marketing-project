## Proposed redesign boundary

Plan a later, separately released change that creates a new ImageGen preflight script and the corresponding carousel visual instructions. The pre-harness base `052b88bc` has no current preflight script or visual manifest. Use the frozen branch's current gate and manifest as read-only references for mapping retained checks; do not cherry-pick that harness or force its manifest into this baseline. Keep implementation limited to the new gate/schema and the documents needed to explain its contract. Do not add a general creative framework, provider, API, scheduler or image-processing path.

Represent three concerns independently:

1. **Campaign contract:** approved locale, customer-visible copy and text limits, source/proof pins, card IDs/order, intended call groupings/reuse, output requirements and any campaign-specific visual commitments.
2. **Style references:** each reference has a pinned path and SHA-256 plus an explicit role such as palette, brand asset or lighting. A style-only reference may not contribute copied labels, record data, layout or physical composition.
3. **Per-card visual concept:** a source-safe scene or diagram summary tied to that card's reader question/action, the intended objects/relationships, and a whitelist for any intentional artwork labels. For example, an F2 forecast-change card might show a linked-lot review; an F3 partner-handoff card might show information traveling with a lot; a P3 audit-request card might show review of a relevant record subset. These are visual intentions only, not facility, product or outcome claims. The concept guides the picture without fixing a universal prop list or layout.

Proposed implementation file remains `scripts/verify_imagegen_preflight.py`; it is not added in this step. `operations/LinkedIn_ImageGen_Preflight_Schema.md` now holds the authorized step-1 design proposal, not executable code or an approved release. Per-call inputs should identify campaign contract and card targets, style-reference paths and SHA-256 values with roles, per-card concept and approved visible-text fields, exact planned prompt text/hash, declared reuse/call coverage and planned output path/ID. The preflight should emit a receipt/token bound to the input spec hash. A separate actual-dispatch record should bind the exact prompt and reference paths/hashes to that receipt. Contract, copy and review-attestation revisions/hashes must match a separately trusted authorized release and be rechecked immediately before dispatch; editing both call inputs and their embedded criteria cannot replace that release. Expected call count and reuse groups come from that pinned campaign contract. The current 10-record example uses eight selected images and A1=B1/A5=B5; these are current campaign settings, not universal gate constants or a default eight-call ceiling.

Keep the lifecycle staged: **PREGEN** validates the content/reference/concept spec and planned targets/output path; **DISPATCH** records the exact prompt, references and preflight receipt for the actual call; **POSTGEN** checks original native bytes, dimensions, manifest/source-map identity and human semantic review. Actual output hashes/dimensions are POSTGEN evidence, not preflight assertions. Do not put the generated preflight receipt inside its own hashed input spec.

## Gate policy

**Hard, mechanical checks** should cover schema validity; exact approved customer fields and their source/proof pins; campaign-specific channel and punctuation rules; an approved visible-text whitelist that excludes internal safeguards and unapproved values; required source/proof pin presence and hashes plus required source/copy review-attestation presence; reference existence/hash/role; prompt-to-call hash binding; target coverage/reuse exactly as declared in the contract; and unique planned output paths. POSTGEN checks separately cover original native-file provenance, byte integrity, dimensions and manifest/source-map identity. These checks can verify that required pins and reviewer attestations are present and match declared bytes; they cannot deterministically prove semantic factual support or that a claim is supported by its source. Reserve that judgment for a human source-and-copy review. A valid preflight means inputs satisfy mechanical checks. It does not authorize generation, certify generated words, prove semantic diversity or accept brand fidelity.

**Advisory checks** may report repeated concept identifiers, nearly identical scene descriptions, repeated prop lists or likely composition collisions. They must not call a pack diverse because prompt strings or image hashes are unique, and must not reject a card merely because it uses an intentional, source-safe diagram. A declarative concept ID can help a reviewer compare cards but cannot prove what the raster depicts.

**Human post-generation review** must inspect the actual rasters individually and in a contact sheet across the whole scenario and adjacent topics. For each card, the reviewer records whether the visual expresses the card's question/action; for the set, whether meaningful concept variety exists beyond changes in object position, paper shape or copy. Keep style consistent while allowing different visual forms such as a source-safe diagram, object relationship, material scene or other concept selected for the story. If meaning or diversity is unclear, mark `CREATIVE_QUALITY_FAIL` or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`; do not infer a pass from preflight or fixture behavior. Check the generated raster wordmark separately against the authorized mark.

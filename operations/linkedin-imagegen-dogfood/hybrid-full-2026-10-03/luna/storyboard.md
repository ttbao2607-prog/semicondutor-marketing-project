## Cross-campaign consistency

Both carousels use the frozen white, cobalt, navy, cyan, and charcoal palette; bright neutral daylight; soft grounded shadows; photographic material depth; and diagrams integrated into the same physical scene. Keep the supplied Digiwin mark, category treatment, and 1/5–5/5 order stable. The four bound campaign references transfer only the approved visual grammar, and the logo reference transfers only the brand mark. Do not copy reference wording, values, exact compositions, or mandatory props.

Fabless Planning uses an abstract chip-design cue with product, planning-period, and outsourced-lot relationships. It never needs OSAT trays, factory scenes, facility evidence, or invented operational statuses. Partner Architecture uses system ownership, handoff, reconciliation, and responsibility forms on an abstract semiconductor material base; it does not depict an implemented connector or assume which product is authoritative.

The two series share hierarchy and materials without repeating one fixed tabletop or prop arrangement: the Fabless sequence moves from an oblique forecast ribbon to a top-down scope, close source/time planes, an open confirmation gap, and a deeper synthesis scene; the Partner sequence moves from a boundary seam to an asymmetric owner/copy close-up, diagonal reconciliation channels, a receding confirmation sequence, and an elevated open rail. Openings orient, middle cards clarify, and endings synthesize without claiming a universal workflow.

Use only each card's exact approved artwork fields, labels, and the official brand mark as raster wording. Caption, native headline, alt, and internal notes remain outside the image. F2-A5 reserves room for its approved Taiwan context and CTA; P2-A5 reserves a separate readable area for the exact approved Taiwan source and CTA. These layouts still need visual, mobile-legibility, copy-rendering, and source-scope review after generation.

## Structure: 3 ads per journey
The journey viewers show three stages before the reader, and the route is Cold Single image → RMK Carousel. Each stage has one feed caption, so each stage maps to one ad:

| Role | Format | Cards | Stage | Reading of the data |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `COLD_SINGLE_IMAGE` | Single image | 1 | cold | First touch; objective Brand awareness (route decision) |
| `EXPLAIN_CAROUSEL` | Carousel | 5 (6 for the 9 Fabless journeys) | explanation | RMK carousel that explains the problem; the Fabless sixth card is "Consulting and implementation in Vietnam" |
| `PROOF_CAROUSEL` | Carousel | 4 | proof | RMK carousel with the case; closing card leads to the reader |

All 99 stages have exactly one caption (no stage carries two different intro texts), all journey images are 1254 × 1254, and every closing card carries a CTA label.

## Where each field comes from
| Field | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `intro_text`, card IDs | the journey data (stage caption) | Fact (from repo) |
| `card_native_headlines` vs `card_artwork_headlines`, `card_alt_texts` | journey fields `native_headline`, `headline` (the text inside the artwork) and `alt`; the cards file also keeps body, source line, CTA label and image path per card | Fact. **The native headline is the platform card/ad headline candidate; the artwork headline is the text printed in the image.** They differ on 68 of 339 cards (whitespace normalised), for example O3: artwork "What is holding up month-end?" vs native "Which factory records are still missing?" |
| `closing_card_cta_label` | CTA text on the last card | Fact; this is the text inside the artwork. The LinkedIn CTA **button** is a separate platform field (see D2) |
| `objective_proposal`, `audience_proposal` | route decisions and RMK pack ([01](../../linkedin-rmk-source-readiness/2026-10-08/01_RMK_Source_Rule_Decision_Pack.md), [03](../../linkedin-rmk-source-readiness/2026-10-08/03_RMK_Fallback_Decision_Tree.md)) | Proposal |
| `destination_proposal` | all ads → the reader route of the journey; cold and explanation ads follow PO decision D1=A (12/10/2026) | Proof: fact from inventory. Cold/explanation: **PO decision, PROVISIONAL until the manager approves** ([record](../2026-10-12/PO_Decision_Record_D1_D6_2026-10-12.md)) |
| `utm_*_pattern`, `ad_key` | stage-aware pattern from [04 mapping](../../linkedin-rmk-source-readiness/2026-10-08/04_Reader_Entry_Mapping_Draft.md); `cold` for the Single image, `rmk` for both carousels | Proposal (no LinkedIn UTM convention exists in the repo; inventory first) |
| `source_ref` | case and anchor documents per treatment | Fact (reference only) |

Intro text length across the 99 ads: minimum 38, median 61, maximum 230 characters (cold 42/64/208, explanation 41/68/230, proof 38/54/171). LinkedIn's field limits and the carousel card-count limit are **not documented in the repository**, so they were not checked; verify in the platform UI before any upload.

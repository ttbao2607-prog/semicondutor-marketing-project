## Shared source behavior

One idempotent initializer per route is the sole route observer for the route’s disjoint section IDs. It uses `IntersectionObserver({threshold: 0, rootMargin: '-45% 0px -45% 0px'})`, emits one `section_view` with exactly `event` and `section_name`, and emits `section_engagement_time` with exactly `event`, `section_name`, and integer `engagement_seconds` 1–3600. Timing accumulates only while a section is intersecting, the document is visible, and the window is focused. Visibility/blur pauses; visible/focus resumes; pagehide performs at most one bounded flush per viewed section. No engagement event is sent below one second. Source tests cover the boundaries and forbidden payload/event families.

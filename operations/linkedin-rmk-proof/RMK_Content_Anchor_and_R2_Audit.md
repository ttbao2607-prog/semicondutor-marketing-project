## Cause and correction anchor

Writer selected the completed Quality cold pack, then avoided unsupported same-pain case claims by selecting PR01 general industry context. Scope caution was justified; replacing all case substance with that context was not. The original branch objective was consideration through success/case/international proof. Production criteria tested traceability/readability better than **new information gained by a warm reader**. Image generation carried the unchanged weak copy forward. Do not spend another image iteration until the copy contains the missing evidence payload.

**Cold job:** establish industry understanding through a recognizable operating situation. **RMK job:** give a warm reader concrete, source-backed reasons to consider Digiwin and a way to examine them. Continuity is situation → supplier evidence → verifiable next step, not repeated pain/diagram/visual style.

Future RMK brief/copy must answer these before images:

1. **Digiwin là ai trong bài toán này?** State supplier/category and role clearly, grounded in sources. Company background may support this, not substitute for case substance. Do not merge ERP and MES capabilities or claim all results came from ERP alone.
2. **Có kinh nghiệm/proof cụ thể nào?** Give at least one identifiable case/entity with market, relevant operating context, Digiwin/solution role and implementation scope. A success label/result requires actual published outcome evidence and permitted scope; a qualitative implementation case is acceptable if no reliable metric exists.
3. **Tôi có thể tự kiểm tra gì?** Visible case name/title and exact searchable entity spelling, plus source/publisher/resource locator in copy/native fields or destination as appropriate. Do not leave essential proof solely behind a generic “đọc thêm”. A URL is not required in every image; usable search terms and case identity are.
4. **Điều này liên quan gì và tôi nên làm gì tiếp?** Explain same-pain versus adjacent evidence honestly; next step names the case/resource to examine. Do not assume buying intent or force consultation. Ad must stand alone even under warm hypothesis.

Warm-reader acceptance test: after the ad, reviewer can name Digiwin's role and a specific case, state what that case supports and its market/scope, and reproduce a reasonable search/resource locator. Reviewer should point to at least one material fact absent from the selected cold ad. If these are absent, mark AD_COPY_FAILED before ImageGen. PO editorial review does not become buyer memory/brand-lift evidence.
